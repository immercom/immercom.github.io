ImmerCom 2025

The 3rd ACM Workshop on Mobile Immersive Computing, Networking, and Systems

To be held in conjunction with ACM MobiCom 2025

November 8th, 2025, Hong Kong, China

Welcome

The program committee is delighted to invite you to the 3rd ACM Workshop on Mobile Immersive Computing, Networking, and Systems (ImmerCom 2025). The workshop will be held on November 8, 2025, in conjunction with MobiCom 2025.

Please kindly note that in-person participation at this workshop is strongly encouraged, but remote presentations can be made available upon request by the authors of accepted papers.

Important Dates

  • Paper submission deadline: July 19th, 2025, AoE, August 8th, 2025, AoE
  • Paper acceptance notification: August 12th, 2025, AoE, September 1st, 2025, AoE
  • Camera-ready due: September 13th, 2025, AoE
  • Workshop Date: November 8th, 2025
